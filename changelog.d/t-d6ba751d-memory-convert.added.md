- **Lernvorschlag wird Fall.** In the memory detail sheet, editors get „Fall daraus machen“ on a pending learning suggestion, placed before „Ablehnen“. It opens the case form with a „Ausgangspunkt“ block: the suggestion is quoted with agent, count and date, the fields stay empty, and „In ‚Erwartet‘ übernehmen“ copies the quote into the expected-behaviour field. The case is created via `POST /agents/{agent_id}/memories/{memory_id}/convert`; the sheet stays open and shows „Zu Fall geworden“ with a „Zum Fall“ link. A suggestion that was rejected or converted in the meantime shows its own message. The case form also accepts an old-feedback or pattern starting point for the upcoming entries.
