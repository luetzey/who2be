Your agent gets better when you say what is off and check whether the change works. On your agent's page the **Evolution** tab shows the four steps and what is waiting for you.

## ① Collect [#sammeln]

Report a case when the agent gets something wrong. Agents report cases themselves too. How it works is explained in [Feedback and cases](/help/feedback-and-cases#rueckmeldung).

Assign cases to a building block, for example the playbook that was wrong. Triaged cases are ready for the review.

## ② Discuss [#besprechen]

Click **Prepare a review**:

1. Choose the cases. One to three are ideal, more make the review long.
2. Who2Be puts together the situation: open cases, uses and signals per version.
3. Click **Start in your client** and hold the review in Claude with the Builder.

The Builder suggests changes. You decide. Agreed changes then show up in the next step.

## ③ Implement [#umsetzen]

Every agreed change becomes a draft on the building block, such as a new version of the playbook. Check the draft and activate it. Only then does the agent work with the new version. The related cases now show **Addressed**.

## ④ Check [#pruefen]

On the agreed date the follow-up check appears under "To do". There you answer "Did it work?".

- **Yes:** the case shows **Verified**.
- **No:** the case is open again and the cycle starts over.

## Test cases [#prueffaelle]

A case can become a test case. It describes the input and the expected behaviour. The agent's **Test cases** tab collects them. That way you check with every new version whether old mistakes come back.

Tip: small reviews often beat one big review rarely. The **Patterns** tab under **Feedback** shows what repeats, see [Patterns](/help/feedback-and-cases#muster).
