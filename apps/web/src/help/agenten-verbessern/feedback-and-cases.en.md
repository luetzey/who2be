Your agent gets better when you say what is off. There are two ways: short signals on a building block and cases about a specific situation. You find both in the navigation under **Feedback**.

## Signals on a building block [#signale]

A signal is a short rating of a building block, that is a persona, a playbook or a resource: **Helpful**, **Outdated**, **Incorrect** or **Unclear**, plus an optional note.

- Agents give signals themselves when they notice something while working.
- You give a signal on the building block's page with **Give feedback**.

The **Building blocks** tab shows per building block how often it was used and how the signals look. That tells you which building block needs care.

## Report a case [#rueckmeldung]

A case describes a specific situation in which an agent should have acted differently. Under **Feedback**, click **Report a case**, choose the agent and fill in these fields:

- **Situation:** What was going on? For example "customer asks for an invoice from 2024".
- **What it did:** What did the agent do?
- **Expected:** What should it have done?
- **Impact (optional):** What happened as a result?

Don't paste passwords or customer data. Agents can also report cases themselves when they notice that something is off.

## What happens to a case [#ablauf]

Every case has a status:

1. **Open:** newly reported.
2. **Triaged:** assigned to a building block, for example the playbook that was wrong.
3. **In progress:** a change to the building block has been agreed.
4. **Addressed:** the new version is active.
5. **Verified:** the follow-up check confirmed that the change helps.

If a case doesn't fit, you can **dismiss** it. If the problem comes back, it moves to **Reopened**. A case can also become a test case that checks every new version of the agent.

## Patterns [#muster]

In the **Patterns** tab Who2Be counts similar cases and lessons. From a fixed number on, a pattern appears, for example "Similar cases 3×". Who2Be doesn't interpret anything, it only counts. What you do with it is up to you.

## Problems with Who2Be itself [#problem-melden]

If something in Who2Be itself doesn't work, such as a tool call or a page, use **Report a problem**. That isn't a case about your agent but a report to us.

How cases turn into a better version is explained in [Evolve your agents](/help/evolution).
