In four steps you connect Who2Be to Claude and build your first agent of your own. Who2Be checks off finished steps by itself.

## Your first agent is ready [#builder-ready]

The Builder is already here. It helps you build your own agents in chat and needs nothing else.

## Connect to Claude [#connect-claude]

Open the Builder under **Agents** and connect it to Claude. Then send Claude a test sentence:

```
Who are you? Load your persona from Who2Be.
```

As soon as the Builder loads its persona, this step is checked off.

## Build your own agent [#build-own-agent]

Tell the Builder in Claude what your agent is for. It asks what it needs to know and creates the agent in Who2Be. Then connect the new agent as in the previous step. Its first fetch checks off this step.

## Learn how your agent gets better [#learn-feedback]

Agents report feedback and cases to Who2Be. The help page on this explains in three minutes how to turn them into better playbooks.
