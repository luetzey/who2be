In Who2Be you decide who your AI agents are: what they are called, how they work and what they know. Claude and other programs fetch this from here in every conversation. When you change something in Who2Be, your agent already works with it on the next fetch.

## What an agent is made of [#bausteine]

An agent is built from a few building blocks:

- **Persona:** role, tone and limits. It tells the agent who it is.
- **Playbooks:** step-by-step procedures for recurring tasks.
- **Resources:** knowledge the agent looks up when it needs it.
- **System prompt:** the template that brings everything together.

Every building block has versions. You can always see what changed and bring back an older version.

## Your first agent is already here [#builder]

Under **Agents** the **Builder** is waiting for you. It is fully set up and helps you build your own agents in chat. You describe what your agent is for. The Builder asks follow-up questions and creates the building blocks in Who2Be.

## How Claude picks up your agent [#abruf]

1. You connect an agent to Claude once. It takes about two minutes.
2. In every new chat the agent loads its persona from Who2Be.
3. When it needs a playbook or a resource, it fetches just that part.

The page [Connect to Claude](/help/connect-claude) explains exactly how to connect.
